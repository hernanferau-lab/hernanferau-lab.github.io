ICT Business Analyst · MVP Delivery

Asset Lifecycle Decision Engine (MVP)

Sole BA and MVP project manager on a fleet and asset management SaaS for Australian councils, taking it from a backlog of open defects to its pilot phase.

Railz Asset Management 2025 - present
Requirements BPMN & Data Flow Mapping UAT Stakeholder Engagement Launch Readiness

Problem

A boutique consulting firm needed to move from spreadsheet-based fleet assessments to a scalable software product. The team needed clarity on what to build first, how workflows should behave, and how business rules should be encoded. After the first build, the backlog held about 290 items and 62 open defects, with a first council demo coming up.

Approach

Drafted structured requirements (SRS, FRS, functional architecture briefs), mapped processes in BPMN, documented 7 end-to-end workflows and modelled data flows across three DFD levels. During stabilisation I grouped defects into 4 root-cause families, ran UAT with real data from two councils, and signed off items only when acceptance criteria, comment history, code and a fresh staging run all agreed. I also prepared release readiness and a Council Engagement Playbook for the first demo, and built the landing page used to capture leads at an industry event. Aligned to ISO 31000, ISO 55001 and IPWEA NAMS+.

Outcome

Open defects went from 62 to 30 in about a week, 60+ items were signed off and 20+ returned with evidence, and the MVP moved into its pilot phase with councils. A 12-week internship converted into an ongoing contract based on the value delivered.

Confidential engagement. Internal scoring logic, weights and client data are not shown here.

In-house BA · Pricing & Forecasting

Pricing Strategy & Sales Forecasting

Two years as in-house analyst for a women's fashion e-commerce brand, extended into a Master's capstone modelling discount strategy and forecasting seasonal revenue.

Paes The Label 2023 - 2025
Power BI SARIMA Causal Inference Scenario Analysis

Problem

Between 2023 and 2024 the brand's average discount rose 85.6%, while sales fell 52.4%. Discounting had become the default lever for demand, and no one could see whether it was actually working, or which categories were being damaged by it.

Approach

Built Power BI dashboards to make category-level margins and inventory visible. For the capstone, extended the analysis with a causal-inference model (Causal Forest / CATE) to measure discount impact by category, and a SARIMA forecast to project peak-season revenue under four discount scenarios.

Outcome

Identified categories where discounts destroyed margin (coats, dresses) versus those where targeted 10-25% discounts lifted revenue (crop tops, sets, pants). The "no discount" scenario projected the highest peak-season revenue of the four options tested.

Academic modelling based on real workplace data. Numbers describe forecast scenarios, not deployed results.

Applied AI · RAG Prototype

AI-Powered Legal Assistant

Deployed a Retrieval-Augmented Generation prototype that turns a procedural document into an interactive Q&A assistant grounded strictly in its source content.

Live demo ↗ GitHub ↗
Python LangChain FAISS Streamlit OpenAI API

Problem

Complaint-handling procedures live in long documents that few people read end to end, leading to inconsistent decisions and heavy reliance on expert interpretation.

Approach

Built a RAG pipeline: chunk the source document, embed with OpenAI, index in FAISS, retrieve the top-k fragments per question, and generate answers constrained to that retrieved context, always returning the source fragments used, for traceability.

Outcome

A live prototype where users can ask natural-language questions and get grounded answers with visible source excerpts. Shows how a static procedure can become an auditable decision-support tool.

Simulated source content based on the principles of Chile's Ley Karin (Law 21.643). Not a replacement for professional advice.
